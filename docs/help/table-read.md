# The Table Read

View > Table Read shows the script to be read aloud around a table, or on a TV or a projector: large type on dark, one scene at a time, each actor's lines in a tint of their own. It reads the script and changes nothing in it; what it keeps is what you picked, while it is open.

On a desktop it is a window of its own, to drag to a second screen (F11 or the full-screen button fills that screen with it). In the browser and on a phone it is a tab in the middle beside the script's, with a **Table Read** tab on the ribbon (a Table Read menu in the Menus look); its full-screen button fills the page, which Chrome's own Cast (⋮ > Cast…, Cast tab) sends to a TV.

## Reading

- **Next** and **Previous** (→ and ←, Page Down and Page Up, Space) go to the next and the previous scene picked. ↑ and ↓ scroll the scene.
- The line under the title says how long the scene is, in eighths of a page, and roughly how long it takes to read (a page a minute), and the same for everything picked. When every frame of the scene has a time (a `[[board: …, duration: 2.5]]` note, or Storyboarder's boards, which always have one), the scene is as long as its boards ("by its boards"); when only some do, it is never shorter than they add up to.
- Tap a line to mark it as the one being read: a screen following this one goes there too (below).

## Picking what is read

**Pick** opens the panel:

- **From** and **To**: a range of scenes.
- **Tag**: only the scenes with a breakdown element (Props: lantern), on a shoot day of the schedule (Shoot day 2), or of a scene colour.
- The cast: **Everyone**, or one character's scenes only (where they speak). Each name has its cast photo from the Characters pane (`photo:` in the `/* Characters */` block) and a bar in their tint.
- **Narrator**: the headings, the action and the transitions read by one voice, marked NARRATOR where the narrator takes over. Off, they are shown plainly.
- **Storyboard**: the scene's storyboard frames between its lines (`[[board: …]]` notes, Storyboarder's boards on its heading).
- **Beside**: the frames beside the lines instead, each by the line it follows (a `[[board:]]` note illustrates the line above it; Storyboarder's boards stand by the heading), with its time under it. The lines keep their place, so you read on while the boards run down the side.
- **Actor tints**: each actor's lines in a colour of their own, the same in every scene (by the order they first speak). A character's cast photo is also beside their first cue.

The picks combine: Anna's scenes on shoot day 2, from scene 10 on.

## A screen that follows yours

The Table Read can follow another device, so a phone or a laptop drives the TV:

1. On the device you read from, **Copy the Follow Link** (Table Read tab, Collaboration menu, or the Session tab). The script is shared first if it is not ([Editing together](index.md)).
2. Open the link on the TV's browser (or give it to Accolade on the computer by the projector). It opens the Table Read and follows the first person there who leads, scene and line.
3. Read on your own device: its Table Read's scene and the line you tap go to the TV; with its Table Read closed, the line your cursor is on.

The screen opened with the follow link only follows: nothing typed there reaches the script (it is put back), and its language, format and source stay its own. Others see it as a viewer, and nobody follows it. Any Table Read can follow someone too: **Follow** in the panel, or Follow on the Table Read tab.
