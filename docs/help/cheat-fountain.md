# Fountain Syntax

One line per element. Blank lines matter: most elements need one before them. Full page: [Fountain Reference](fountain.md).

## Script

- **Title page** — `Title: The Long Rain` at the top; a blank line ends it
- **Scene heading** — `INT. KITCHEN - NIGHT` (also EXT., INT./EXT., I/E, EST.)
- **Forced heading** — `.FLASHBACK`
- **Scene number** — `INT. KITCHEN - NIGHT #12A#`
- **Action** — any other paragraph: `Rain against the window.`
- **Forced action** — `!BANG. The door slams.`
- **Character** — `ANNA` in capitals, a blank line above, dialogue right under
- **Forced character** — `@McBRIDE`
- **Extension** — `ANNA (V.O.)`
- **Dialogue** — the lines right under the cue: `Is it still raining?`
- **Parenthetical** — `(quietly)` inside a speech
- **Dual dialogue** — `TOM ^` on the second cue
- **Lyrics** — `~Row, row, row your boat`
- **Transition** — `CUT TO:` or `> FADE OUT.`
- **Centred** — `> THE END <`
- **Page break** — `===`

## Emphasis

- **Italic** — `*word*` (Ctrl+I)
- **Bold** — `**word**` (Ctrl+B)
- **Bold italic** — `***word***`
- **Underline** — `_word_` (Ctrl+U)

## Not printed

- **Note** — `[[Check the date.]]`
- **Boneyard** — `/* … */` around the lines to leave out
- **Section** — `# ACT ONE`, `## The flood`
- **Synopsis** — `= Anna decides to leave.`

## Element keys

- **Ctrl+1 … Ctrl+8** — Scene Heading, Action, Character, Parenthetical, Dialogue, Transition, Centered, Lyrics
- **Tab** — on an empty line: a character cue; in dialogue: a parenthetical
