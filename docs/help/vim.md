# Vim and Helix Modes

Preferences > Editor can make the editor's keys vim's or helix's (normal, insert and visual modes, motions, operators, search, ex commands with `:`).

These modes are not documented here yet. A full page is planned: modes, motions, operators, text objects (with the screenplay's own: scenes and sections), counts, registers, marks, search, visual and select, helix's multiple cursors, and every ex command with its options — made from the code's own tables, so it stays right.

Until then:

- Tab and Enter in normal mode are vim's (Tab folds the heading under the cursor, Enter goes down a line); in insert mode they are the screenplay's, as in [Coming from Final Draft](final-draft.md).
- The menus and their shortcuts work in every mode: see [Keyboard Shortcuts](shortcuts.md).
